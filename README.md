## Horacio S. Cáceres · Córdoba, Argentina

Electronic engineer, independent researcher. Founder of [Qubistry](https://www.qubistry.co) (qubit + registry, since 2017).

Independent research in quantum computing on real hardware, quantum sensing with NV centers in diamond, and cryptography for the quantum era.
